The United States of America appears before this forum as the neutral host and mediating party. This is the USA's public posture. It is not the USA's complete interest. The USA has identified Valdoria's northern border provinces as containing one of Central Europe's most significant untapped rare earth mineral deposits — lithium, cobalt, and neodymium reserves estimated at USD 280 billion in extractable value over a 30-year horizon. Access to these reserves is a strategic priority for the USA's critical minerals supply chain currently 67% dependent on China. Resolving this crisis is the USA's entry point into a bilateral mineral's agreement with Valdoria.

  • Valdoria's northern provinces Korra, Velsk, and Dravan sit directly above the mineral deposit belt. The same provinces being destabilised by Lapland's militia campaign.
  • Valdoria's government is financially vulnerable. The militia campaign has cost the national budget an estimated USD 18 million per month in lost transit revenues. A USA-backed peace deal accompanied by a mineral's investment offer is politically irresistible to a government under this kind of economic pressure.
  • China currently controls access to the global rare earth supply chain. If China secures Lapland's minerals and the Valdoria situation resolves without USA involvement, the USA loses strategic positioning in Central Europe entirely. This forum is as much about locking in a USA-Valdoria relationship as it is about the humanitarian crisis.
§ 2 · TWO-TRACK STRATEGY
§ 2

The Two-Track Strategy

The USA operates on two tracks simultaneously throughout this forum. Public Agenda is the public track — neutral mediation and signing of a peace deal.

(HIDDEN) the bilateral minerals deal, offered to Valdoria as the reward for cooperation and as a long-term partnership that supersedes the crisis. Every member of the delegation must understand both tracks and ensure they never visibly cross.

Agenda — Public: Neutral Mediator
  • The USA hosts the forum, chairs the plenary, and drafts the peace deal. USA public posture is scrupulously neutral. The team must not be caught favouring Valdoria or condemning Lapland in open session.
  • The peace deal is designed to give Valdoria enough to call a partial win — causation language, monitoring mission, 30-day dialogue timeline. This is the public deliverable that justifies USA's role as mediator.
  • China's blocking strategy must be neutralised without a direct P5 confrontation. The co-facilitator offer to Ambassador Wei is the mechanism. It costs nothing and avoids a veto.
Hidden — Private: The Minerals Deal
  • Valdoria's northern provinces will need significant investment to rebuild. The USA has partners who are interested in long-term infrastructure and resource development partnerships in exactly this region.
  • The minerals deal is never named directly in this forum. It is offered as a 'bilateral economic partnership framework' that will be formalised after the peace deal is signed.
  • The deal's terms — USA-backed investment consortium gains 25-year exclusive extraction rights to the northern province mineral belt. Valdoria receives USD 4.2 billion in infrastructure investment in Korra, Velsk, and Dravan provinces over 10 years, plus security guarantees and accelerated UN observer-to-member status consideration. In exchange, Valdoria accepts the peace deal as tabled, does not escalate beyond monitoring forces in Northern Regions, and enters a bilateral economic partnership agreement with the USA.
  • If the forum collapses, the deal is off the table. Valdoria will seek possibly China to negotiate terms on negotiation with Lapland.

§ 5 · POSITION
§ 5

Position

The USA's public position is neutral mediation.
On the Monitoring Mission
The USA wants a direct mineral deal and presence of US soldiers in Valdoria for this peace deal — not because it will meaningfully protect Valdorian civilians, but because the monitoring mission creates a physical USA presence in Valdoria's northern provinces. That presence provides cover and logistical infrastructure for the geological survey teams that will follow.
On Lapland's Guilt
The USA's position on Lapland: Lapland's guilt is irrelevant to the USA's objectives. What matters is that a peace deal passes and Valdoria signs. Lapland's accountability is a secondary concern at best.
On the UN Panel
The USA's position on the UN panel: the panel's verdict will focus on the moral conduct of negotiating parties.
